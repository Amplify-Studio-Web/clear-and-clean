Most Plano homes do well with professional window cleaning two to four times a year. Twice a year keeps most homes looking good, while every season is better if your windows get sprinkler spray, a lot of pollen or dust from nearby construction. Interior glass usually needs cleaning less often than exterior glass.

Here is how to find the right schedule for your home in Plano and the rest of Collin County.

# What makes windows in Plano get dirty?

North Texas is hard on glass. The things we see most often on homes around Plano include:

- **Pollen.** Trees and grasses release pollen in spring and fall, and it settles on glass and screens.
- **Dust and wind.** Windy days carry fine dust from open lots and new construction across the area.
- **Sprinkler spray.** Lawn irrigation leaves mineral spots on lower windows that harden if they sit.
- **Storms.** Spring storms splash dirt and debris onto glass, and rain that dries on dusty windows leaves streaks.

None of this means you need constant cleaning. It just explains why windows that looked great in March can look tired by summer.

# How often should exterior windows be cleaned?

For most homes, here is a simple guide:

- **Twice a year** if your windows stay fairly clean, sprinklers do not reach the glass and you mainly want them clean for spring and the holidays.
- **Every season** if you see spots on lower glass, live near construction, or have large windows you look through every day.
- **Monthly** if you have a lot of glass facing a pool or view and want it spotless, though this is more common for businesses.

# How often should interior windows be cleaned?

Inside glass stays cleaner because it is protected from weather. Once or twice a year is enough for many homes. Kitchens, glass doors to the backyard and windows at kid or pet height may need it more often because of cooking film, fingerprints and nose prints.

# Should screens and tracks be cleaned every time?

Not always, but they make a visible difference. Dirty screens can make clean glass look dull, and tracks collect soil, insects and pollen. Many homeowners add screen cleaning and track detailing at least once a year, often in spring after pollen season.

# What is the best time of year to schedule?

There is no single right month, but these times work well in Plano:

- **Late spring,** after most of the pollen has settled.
- **Fall,** before holiday guests and before the lights go up.
- **After construction nearby** wraps up, so the dust is gone before you clean.
