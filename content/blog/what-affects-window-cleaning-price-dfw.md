Window cleaning prices in DFW depend mostly on how many panes you have, what style they are, how easy they are to reach, and which services you choose. Adding interior cleaning, screens or tracks changes the price, and recurring plans lower the cost of each visit. The quickest way to get an exact number is a quote based on your home.

Here is what goes into a quote, so you know what to expect before you ask for one.

# Why does the number of panes matter most?

Pane count is the starting point for most residential quotes because it reflects how much glass there is to clean. A small home with a handful of windows takes far less time than a large home with walls of glass, French doors and transoms.

That is why our [free quote](/contact) form starts by asking how many window panes you have. You pick the range that best fits your property, from a small to average home up to a large estate or commercial property.

# How do window style and size change the price?

Not all panes take the same effort. Some things that add time:

- **Divided-light windows.** Windows with grids or many small panes have more edges and corners to detail.
- **Oversized glass.** Large picture windows and glass walls take longer to clean streak-free.
- **Specialty shapes.** Arched, round and transom windows can be harder to reach and work around.

# How does access affect a quote?

Reaching the glass safely is a big part of the job. Ground-floor windows are straightforward. Second-story and high windows need ladders or other professional access tools, and steep slopes, landscaping or tight side yards can make setup slower.

Our exterior service includes professional access for high windows, so you do not have to worry about the hard-to-reach glass. It simply factors into the time the job takes.

# What add-on services change the total?

Exterior window cleaning is included in every instant quote. From there, you can add:

- **Interior window cleaning** for fingerprints, cooking film, pet marks and dust inside.
- **Screen cleaning** to remove dust, pollen and buildup that make clean glass look dull.
- **Track detailing** for the channels, sills and corners where dirt and insects collect.

Each one is optional, and you choose what makes sense for your home.

# Does the condition of the glass matter?

It can. Glass that has not been cleaned in a long time, or that has heavy mineral spots from sprinklers, takes more work than glass that is cleaned regularly. Construction residue such as paint, mortar or stucco on new windows can also add time. Mineral and hard water remediation is included with our service plans.

# How do recurring plans lower the cost per visit?

We offer seasonal, quarterly and monthly plans. The more often you schedule, the more you save on each visit, and every plan includes priority scheduling. Regular visits also keep buildup from getting heavy, which keeps each cleaning simpler.

# Why don't companies list one flat price?

Every home is different. Two houses with the same square footage can have very different glass, access and needs. A quote based on your actual windows is more accurate than a flat rate, and it avoids surprise fees. Honest pricing, with clear quotes and no pressure, is one of the things we stand for.
