Exterior window cleaning removes the dust, pollen, sprinkler spots and construction residue that build up on the outside of your glass. Interior cleaning removes fingerprints, cooking film, pet marks and dust on the inside. Most DFW homes need the outside cleaned more often, but booking both together gives the clearest view because you are not looking through one dirty side.

Here is how to decide what your home needs.

# What does exterior window cleaning remove?

The outside of your glass takes the most abuse in North Texas. The buildup we see most often includes:

- **Texas dust** blown in on windy days.
- **Pollen** from trees and grasses in spring and fall.
- **Sprinkler spray** that dries into mineral spots on lower panes.
- **Construction residue** in neighborhoods where new homes or roads are going in.

Our exterior process has four steps. We scrub to loosen dirt, pollen and mineral buildup, squeegee for a streak-free finish, detail the edges, corners, frames and sills, and apply our RainDefense finish where it is included. We also clean high and second-story glass with professional equipment and safe methods.

# What does interior window cleaning remove?

Inside the home, the buildup is different. It comes from everyday life:

- **Fingerprints** on lower panes and patio doors.
- **Cooking film** on kitchen windows.
- **Pet marks** where dogs and cats look outside.
- **Dust** that settles on glass and sills.

Our interior process starts by protecting floors and nearby surfaces. We wash the glass to lift film, dust and fingerprints, detail corners, edges and accessible sills, then walk the work with you. We work neatly around furniture, kids and pets, and we carefully work around accessible blinds and shutters.

# Which side gets dirty faster?

For most homes, the outside. Weather, pollen and sprinklers keep adding to exterior glass, while the inside only collects what happens in the house. That is why many homeowners clean the exterior on a regular schedule and add interior cleaning less often.

Some homes are the exception. A kitchen window over the stove, a back door the dog uses all day, or a playroom with small hands on the glass can get dirty inside just as fast.

# When should you book both?

Booking interior and exterior together makes sense when:

- **The glass looks hazy and you cannot tell which side is dirty.** Cleaning both removes the guesswork.
- **You are getting ready for guests, a holiday or a move.** A complete clean makes the biggest difference.
- **It has been a while since either side was cleaned.** A full reset is a good starting point before a regular plan.
- **You want the most natural light.** Clean glass on both sides lets more light into each room.

Most DFW homeowners choose both services for a complete result.

# When is exterior only enough?

An exterior-only visit can work well between full cleanings. If the inside still looks good but sprinkler spots, pollen or dust are showing on the outside, an exterior clean brings the view back without the time inside the home. Dirty screens can make clean glass look dull, and tracks collect soil, insects and pollen. Both are available as add-ons to the same visit, so the whole window looks finished when we leave.
